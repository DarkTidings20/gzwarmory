# GZW Armory — Internal roadmap

Pre-0.5 planning notes for the vendor-ladder site. Not launch content.

## Status

Skeleton on `feat/vendor-skeleton`: Gunny AR L1–L4, Handshake armor (Tasking vs Looting), Lab Rat pouches + med fills. Detailed kits wait on the 0.5 preview (Oct 19) and full release (Oct 22).

## Level 4 tracks

Gunny Level 4 is **not** a single endgame build. It uses three empty tracks to be filled after 0.5:

| Track | Intent | When it's worth it |
|-------|--------|--------------------|
| **Vendor preset** | Off-the-shelf Gunny stock | You want a gun that works without a parts chase. The L403A1 is noted as the "cheat gun" — strong unmodded in 0.4-era info; re-verify after 0.5. |
| **Min-max** | Best stat sheet **per gun model** | You want peak numbers for a specific platform. Delivered as curated per-model builds — **not** a live parts builder. |
| **Drip** | Style once stats are close | The competitive options are near enough that looks decide. |

Placeholders on the site read: *To be built after 0.5 (Oct 19).*

### Why per-model min-max (not a parts builder)

The old interactive builder path is retired (redirected to `/vendors`). Editorial value is opinionated, version-stamped builds per vendor level and per gun model — searchable text with verification badges — rather than another attachment sandbox. Min-max content should ship as discrete model pages/slots (e.g. M4A1 min-max, L403A1 min-max) once 0.5 attachment availability is confirmed.

## Other ladders (skeleton)

- **Handshake** — armor/kit; Tasking vs Looting per level; no gun slots.
- **Lab Rat** — pouches available per level + recommended med fills; no gun slots.
- Remaining vendors — placeholder L1–L4 when guides are added.

## Verification rule

Weapons on the Gunny starter line (CQ A1 → M4A1) can be marked fairly confident. **All attachments** stay Pending 0.5 verification (Oct 19); 0.4 names are greyed last-known hints only. Invent nothing: no unverified stats, prices, or rep thresholds beyond sourced notes.
